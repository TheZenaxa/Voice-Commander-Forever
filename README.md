# VOICE COMMANDER FOREVER

Voice commands for World of Warcraft: WoW Forever.

- **VCF:** English only.
- **VCFI** (Voice Commander Forever International): French, German, Spanish, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Japanese and English.

  **JAPANESE:** At the time of this launch, WoW Forever does not offer the Japanese language. VCFI's Japanese is for Japanese speakers who play WoW Forever in English: you speak your commands in Japanese, and VCFI's screens are in English to match the game.

VCF and VCFI are made, intended, and tested for World of Warcraft: Forever, played through Blizzard's Battle.net app.

VCF and VCFI will be available when they are found to be stable enough for initial release. Until then, the download links in their READ ME files will not work.

## WHY YOUR LANGUAGE MATTERS

Windows speech recognition hears you best in the language it is set to, spoken as a native speaker would. Pick the program that matches the language of your Windows speech recognition.

## AVAILABLE NOW: VOICE LANGUAGE TEST

A free tool that shows how well Windows understands you and how good the translations are, to help you decide whether VCF or VCFI is for you. The phrases in the test are the default starting phrases in the current builds of VCF and VCFI. Download VOICE LANGUAGE TEST.exe and its READ ME here:
