## VCF 0.10.327 and VCFI 0.10.319 (dictation into boxes, Say lines, quiet places)

Published 2026-11-25 14:00 US Mountain Standard Time

- Dictation into mail and Auction House boxes now works, and the tooltip box shows the words as they are typed.
- While dictating: a letter said by itself spells; letters said at a steady pace join into one word, a long pause starts a new word, and "space" types a space. "erase" takes off the last thing said, "clear" empties the box. In a box, number words become numbers and punctuation is left out.
- A dictation key you choose in the Control Panel starts and stops dictation. It is only for dictation and never changes push to talk.
- The letter pads have a 123 key for numbers and a Letters key back.
- Tooltips: the "Say ..." lines now show in Brief Mode too. They are not read aloud unless you tick "Also read the Say lines aloud".
- Mail: an opened mail puts the outline on its item; "select" or "get that" takes it.
- Auction House: an auction row says "select to highlight it, then buy that". "move right" from a row goes to Buyout. After "no" on the game's box, the outline goes back where it was.
- Quiet places: a list of cities and hubs (ticked to start: the six capitals, The Crossroads, Booty Bay, Gadgetzan, Ratchet) where other players' names are not read aloud. NPCs, friends and guildmates still are. "All names, always" now starts off.

## VCF 0.10.301 and VCFI 0.10.301 (arrow glow, reorganised Control Panel, sounds for ore and herbs)

Published 2026-11-24 16:07 US Mountain Standard Time

**Health and safety**
- The installer agreement now has a photosensitive seizure warning: the glow on your arrow can pulse, the pulse is off unless you switch it on, and you can switch it off at any time. The same warning sits beside the setting and in the FAQ.

**Your arrow on the maps (new)**
- A glow in a colour you pick on your arrow on the minimap and on the map (the quest log map too), so it is easy to find. Optional soft pulse: slow, medium or fast (about 3 times a second at most, a fade, never a hard flash). Off and colourless until you choose.

- It opens with the middle empty. Pick a group on the left to see its commands; click it again to clear.

- A path line ("Commands > Interaction > Buying and selling") shows where you are; click a part to go back.
- Settings sit in tabs: Listening, Audio, In the game, Colours, My commands, Characters, Help. What was heard stays in front.
- A command whose name also appears in another group says where.
- The /RELOAD and Open the control panel buttons on the MIC IS box have a bold outline that pulses gently; their colour can be picked (white on dark to start).
- The character list's Update and Bring in buttons are gone: VCF reads every save by itself.
- Drop-downs no longer change their choice when you scroll the page over them.

**Ore, herbs and other things in front of you**
- Each name can have a sound: a game sound (including a murloc), a Windows sound, or your own file. "Do Not Play Sound Cues Here" for silence on one name.

- PLAY previews Windows and your own sounds on your PC. Game sounds cannot be previewed, but they work in the game after a /reload (FAQ).
- The list only offers game sounds your game has.

**Reading aloud**
- The READ button beside the outline's tooltip is dark with a pale orange outline, smaller, and shows the words "read tooltip" (VCF), now also a spoken word.
- "messages read" no longer reads the outline's position ("1 of 58").
- "read quest" stops SPOKEN at once, whatever started it (a zone introduction too).

**Keys and voice**
- "minimap in" and "minimap out" (VCF), with one, two and three for a little, halfway and all the way.
- Shift+F4 is now allowed as a key (F4 with Alt or Ctrl stays blocked).
- SCAN AND REPAIR knows the newer parts: a key you chose that VCF also uses, two dedicated slots on one bar slot, a missing sound file, a stale "keep on a hidden key" tick.

**FAQ**
- When did this project begin? The Voice Commander project began on 6 March 2026.

### New in VCFI 0.1.15

VCFI gets everything VCF got today (VCF 0.10.51 and 0.10.52):

**Your spells come from your own spellbook**
- VCFI now takes every class spell from your character's real WoW Forever spellbook. The old built-in class lists (written for a different version of WoW) are gone, so the list only shows what your character can really use.
- Words you gave to a spell are kept.
- Passive spells and racials (always on, nothing to press) no longer get a key. The game itself says which ones are passive.
- Active racials such as Walk on Air, Skysight and Shatter Curse are now in the list.

**Grouped the way the game does it**
- Spells are grouped like your spellbook: General, one group per talent tree, Talents, and Items.
- Your character's name is in the group, for example "Warrior Gallagar · Spellbook: Arms".
- A Hunter's or Warlock's pet commands sit next to the pet's own abilities.

**Updates itself when you reload**
- Learn a new spell, then /reload twice and keep playing. VCFI picks up your character's new spells, talents and items by itself. VCFI never reloads your game on its own.
- The line under the buttons now counts your character's spells and items, and how many are new.

**Food, drink and potions**
- Food and drink get short words: "eat bread", "eat jerky", "drink water".
- Health potions: "drink aitch pee" or "drink health pot". Mana potions: "drink em pee", "drink mah nah pot" or "drink man nah pot".
- Food or drink you no longer carry is switched off and gives up its key, so it never presses a slot that now holds something else.

**Interact (loot, talk, shop, gathering)**
- VCFI keeps its own Interact key. If you give Interact a key of your own in the game, VCFI adds it as a second key. Only one is ever sent.

**Profession abilities on your bars (ALPHA TESTING)**
- Cooking, First Aid, Basic Campfire, Smelting and the crafting windows are found from your game and shown in "Professions (ALPHA TESTING)". Use /zenvci bars, then /zenvci bars yes, then /reload twice. Fishing works as before.

**Other**
- Close All is removed. It had no key in the game. Say "go back" to close a window.
- Your character's race now comes from the game.
- New FAQ entries about /zenvci bars, /zenvci bars yes and /zenvci undo.

Only in VCFI:

**Your language**
- The new group names, messages and FAQ entries are shown in French, German, Spanish and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional). With English chosen, VCFI looks the same as VCF.
- Food, drink and potion words follow the language VCFI listens in:
  - French: "manger pain", "boire eau", "boire potion de soins", "boire potion de mana"
  - German: "Brot essen", "Wasser trinken", "Heiltrank trinken", "Manatrank trinken"
  - Spanish: "comer pan", "beber agua", "beber poción de salud", "beber poción de maná"
  - Chinese (Simplified): "吃" or "喝" with the item's name, "喝治疗药水", "喝法力药水"
  - Chinese (Traditional): "吃" or "喝" with the item's name, "喝治療藥水", "喝法力藥水"

## Voice Language Test 1.1.0

Published 2026-11-04 00:26 US Mountain Standard Time

The voice language test, version 1.1.0. Download VOICE.LANGUAGE.TEST.exe and its READ ME below, then run the exe. There is no installer and nothing is uploaded. It saves a report on your desktop, and your own list if you use TEST CUSTOM LIST. You choose whether to email your report to vcfireports@gmail.com.

New in 1.1.0:
- The license is shown first: click I AGREE to continue.
- Choose TEST VC WORDS (VCFI's own words) or TEST CUSTOM LIST to type and test your own words, one phrase per line.
- Your own list works like VCFI's words: 20 phrases or fewer are done in one go; a longer list stops after 20 and offers 10 more at a time. Testing every phrase still shows the thank-you screen.
- A Finish button on every phrase lets you stop early and save a report of what you have done.
- Your custom list is saved on your computer for next time, in %APPDATA%\Voice language test\custom list.txt (delete that folder to remove it).
- The report says which test you chose.
