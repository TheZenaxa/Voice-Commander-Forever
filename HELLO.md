# Hello!

I'm TheZenaxa. I'm a legally blind player, and I made Voice Commander Forever so I could play World of Warcraft: Forever with my voice.

## What it's for

VCF lets a visually impaired player put their attention on the game itself, not on reading menus. It keeps the screen clear of the oversized icons we'd otherwise need. Those big icons aren't a style choice: normal-sized ones can't be seen. Every menu or button that becomes a spoken word is one less thing to hunt for, and one less big icon covering the game.

## What it does

- **It sets itself up from your character.** It reads your spells and items and gives each one a voice command and its own key. You can change that command to something else, too: it is whatever works for you, the player. Your own key bindings are never changed.
- **Keyboard and mouse OR Controller:** Commands work through recognized keyboard key combinations that you'd likely never try to use with your clunky ole' hands.
- **It only listens to the game.** Nothing you say can press keys in any other program; it acts only while WoW is in front.
- **It's private and free.** Your speech stays on your PC. Nothing you say is uploaded.
- **It learns your voice.** The voice check, from my own singing project VIRTY-VOX, learns your pitch so other voices (a stream, a TV) can be ignored, or help to at least ignore some of it.
- **It reads quests aloud.** Say "read quest" and it reads the open quest with the narrator voice and rate you choose in WoW's TTS settings.
- **It writes chat for you.** Dictation types your chat, mail and Auction House searches as you speak (experimental).
- **It moves your screen around.** Limited editing moves and sizes pieces of the screen by voice (experimental).
- **VCFI** does all of this in six spoken languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese and Chinese.

## Where to start

1. Pick your program: **VCF** for English, **VCFI** for the other languages.
2. Read its READ ME FIRST.
3. Click its SETUP file.

## Closing remarks and thank you

This is also for people who have limited dexterity or otherwise issues with using a standard keyboard and mouse or even a standard controller. While movement is best handled by the thumb-sticks with a controller or the WASD keys via keyboard and mouse, KBM, there ARE movement commands in Voice Commander. However, that said, it is for anyone that wants to use it to play World of Warcraft: Forever as it is intended to be played. It is extremely customizable: use the vocal shortcuts you want and turn off or remove those you do not. Want just emotes on? Great! Turn off the rest. Want just combat by voice, or combat and a few emotes? Want to be able to read those tool-tips in a bigger box? You can enable or disable just about everything as you like! I built it for what I needed, and added on what I thought might be helpful for others. If you decide to use it, I hope it helps bring your gaming to an even more enjoyable level! And if you couldn't play WoW before, but can now because of VCF or VCFI, please let me know! That is the biggest joy I can know is to know I've helped people enjoy themselves. Thank you so, so much! -Z.
